Flyer Studio – photo credits and sources
=========================================
Every photo built into flyer-editor.html comes from Unsplash or Pexels under its free licence.
Both licences allow commercial use and modification (cropping, background removal), and neither requires
attribution, though crediting photographers is appreciated. They do not allow selling the photos unaltered
or compiling them into a competing stock service.
  Unsplash License: https://unsplash.com/license
  Pexels License:   https://www.pexels.com/license/

Images were resized to at most ~1400–2100 px and recompressed (JPEG/WebP ~80 %).
Cut-outs were made locally with rembg (BRIA RMBG-2.0 model), with a soft drop shadow added.

Not photos (made procedurally for this project, no third-party rights):
  tex_paper, tex_kraft, tex_concrete, tex_washi  – seamless paper/kraft/concrete/rice-paper tiles (FFT noise)
  brush                                          – dry-brush paint stroke (re-tintable in the editor)
  torn paper edges                               – generated vector paths with a fibrous rim

Fonts (embedded, SIL Open Font License 1.1, from Google Fonts): Anton, Bebas Neue, Archivo Black, Shrikhand,
Abril Fatface, Monoton, Playfair Display, DM Serif Display, Fraunces, Cormorant Garamond, Pacifico, Yellowtail, Caveat,
Permanent Marker, Space Grotesk, DM Sans. Japanese characters use the device's system fonts.

The sample names, addresses, phone numbers and prices in the templates are invented placeholders
(e.g. "Smoke & Bun", "Casa Luna", "22 33 44 55"). Replace them with your own.
